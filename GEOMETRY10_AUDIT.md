# Geometry10 reliability and diagnostics revision

The active interfaces remain `PV_experiments.train`, `PV_experiments.evaluate`, `PV_experiments.represent`, and `PV_experiments.advanced_report`. The old standalone SAC `04_evaluate` and MBPO `03_evaluate` CLIs are unsupported because their historical evaluation package is absent. Do not use old six-dimensional workflows or checkpoints for Geometry10. Run `python -m pytest -q geometry10_tests` before training. Use a new experiment name after this source revision; old training cannot be resumed under a new training identity.

Checkpoint recovery commits an exact chronological episode history prefix and checks the evaluation day grid, aggregate and snapshot. Model validation uses empirical hierarchical quantiles from 128 independent diagnostic draws; the existing training-time stochastic model and policy equations are unchanged. Model-based replay now includes the final actionable state. Nonfinite raw ensemble samples fail before clipping. The feasibility envelope (-60 C minimum cell-temperature assumption, 200 W/m2 GHI residual allowance, albedo upper bound 1) is **diagnostic only**, not a guaranteed physical bound; normalized saturated irradiance cannot be decoded exactly. No feasibility rejection or altered reward objective is introduced. Report actual synthetic batch draws and model skill, counterfactual realized one-step reward diagnostics, not policy-optimality guarantees. Confirmed attainment is the final checkpoint of the first consecutive successful sequence; onset is retrospective. Restricted-budget savings include all seeds, with nonattainment explicitly recorded.

Use Linux/WSL for managed-job process locks. `recovery_events.json` records known discarded full episodes; unlogged partial interactions cannot be recovered exactly. A run interrupted before its initial checkpoint is created has no saved training state. Existing incomplete standalone evaluation outputs fail closed. New `manager_provenance.json` and `training_identity.json` contracts prevent silent reuse of mismatched inputs; use separate output/experiment identities instead of overwriting old reports. Preserve the final test partition for final evaluation only.

## Final release hardening

The final installer additionally verifies algorithm/seed/epoch/interaction identity inside trusted checkpoint bytes, committed optimizer-update counts, final evaluated actor equality, complete scheduled output hashes, and evaluation input/output seals. Model-validation output adds common-weather equivalent-pose power differences and descriptive empirical coverage by historical day and observed next-irradiance proxy. Advanced learning reports synthetic replay exposure at confirmation or the censoring budget. Physical preflight exercises a repeatable nonzero motion script and exact pose/reward accounting; its separate nominal zero-motion replay is not a matched policy evaluation.

The primary managed experiment remains perturbed historical weather under the frozen hypothetical error specification. Nominal-only policy evaluation, no-synthetic ablation, stronger baseline and changed historical partitions are separate preregistered scientific decisions. Do not interpret validation-target attainment as test-set equivalence or annual yield superiority. A single paired pilot cannot establish an algorithmic advantage. Use new experiment names after changing source contracts. No old evaluation files are silently migrated.
